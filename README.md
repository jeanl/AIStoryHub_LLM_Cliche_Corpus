# AIStoryHub_LLM_Cliche_Corpus

## Overview

The **LLM Cliché Corpus** is a comprehensive, categorized collection of overused phrases, buzzwords, and linguistic patterns commonly generated by Large Language Models (LLMs). This resource is designed to help writers, developers, and content creators identify and avoid repetitive, clichéd language that frequently appears in AI-generated text.

## Purpose

LLMs tend to gravitate toward certain linguistic patterns, metaphors, and phrases during training and generation. This corpus catalogs these patterns across multiple categories, providing a reference guide for:

- **Writers** seeking to identify and eliminate AI-generated clichés from their work
- **Developers** building tools to detect or filter LLM-generated content
- **Researchers** studying language generation patterns and model biases
- **Content creators** aiming for more authentic, varied, and original prose

## What's Included

The corpus contains **15+ categories** of clichéd language patterns, including:

- **Action/Verb Clichés**: Overused action verbs like "leverage," "harness," "unlock," "empower"
- **Corporate/Inspirational Filler**: Buzzwords like "journey," "mission," "synergize," "paradigm shift"
- **Pseudo-Intellectual Abstraction**: Verbose academic-sounding language like "nexus," "paradigm," "ontology"
- **Tech/Marketing Buzzwords**: Industry jargon like "cutting-edge," "AI-powered," "seamless," "revolutionary"
- **Poetic AI Prose**: Overused literary descriptions like "neon-lit," "whisper," "tapestry," "kaleidoscope"
- **Connector/Scaffold Phrases**: Transition words and filler phrases
- **AI Safety/Philosophical Padding**: Ethics-related buzzwords
- **Emotional Appeal Vocabulary**: Overused emotional language
- **Metaphorical Overload**: Clichéd metaphors and comparisons
- **Visual Adjective Clichés**: Repetitive descriptive words
- **Corporate Tone Hedges**: Softening and qualifying phrases
- **Structural Repetition Clichés**: Common sentence templates
- **Emotional Reaction Templates**: Overused descriptions of feelings and physical reactions
- **Perception & Cognition Loops**: Repetitive thought and awareness patterns
- **Body-Anchor Loops**: Clichéd physical sensations and descriptions
- **Phrase Echoes**: Common phrases that appear in LLM output
- **Genre-Specific Fiction Tropes**: Overused narrative devices and story patterns
- **Overused Names**: Character names frequently chosen by LLMs

## Format

The corpus is provided as a structured JSON file (`llm_cliche_corpus.json`) containing:

- **Categories**: Organized collections of related clichés
- **Items**: Individual phrases or words within each category
- **Phrase Patterns**: Regular expressions for detecting more complex clichéd patterns
- **Stop Phrases**: High-priority phrases commonly flagged as clichéd

## Usage

Use this corpus to:

1. **Audit** AI-generated or AI-assisted content for overused language
2. **Train** detection models to identify LLM-generated text
3. **Inform** writing guidelines and editorial standards
4. **Educate** about common patterns in machine-generated language
5. **Develop** tools that help writers improve authenticity
